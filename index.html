♖♘Tigran Petrosian♘♖

World Chess champion from 1963 to 1969

  • Nicknamed "Iron Tigran" due to his almost-impenetrable defensive playing style, which emphasized safety above all else
  • Soviet Armenian chess master
  • Won the world championship from Mikhail Botvinnik in 1963
